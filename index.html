Glossary

What each number means

Impressions
Times a story card was shown in the NewsBreak feed.
Views
Times a story was opened.
Native views
Views read inside the NewsBreak app, as the sheet reports them.
New followers
Followers gained each day (the sheet’s “Followers” column), added up.
Shares, likes, comments
Reader actions on the stories.
Engagement
Shares + likes + comments.
View rate
Views ÷ impressions: how often a shown card was opened.
Change
(This period − comparison) ÷ comparison. Green is up, red is down.
